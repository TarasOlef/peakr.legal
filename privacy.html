PEAKR Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

PEAKR is operated by Taras Olefirenko in Spain. PEAKR is a lifestyle, nutrition, and fitness app, not a medical device.

Information we process

  • Account identifiers, name, and email supplied through Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Age range, calculation sex, height, weight, nutrition goals, calorie and macro targets, meals, water entries, and step data you choose to connect or provide.
  • Meal descriptions, food photos and progress photos you explicitly capture or select, and feedback.
  • Subscription product, entitlement, purchase status, renewal, and expiration information. Apple processes payment details.
  • App version, device information, feature interactions, session events, and technical errors needed to operate and improve PEAKR.

How we use information

We use it to authenticate and sync your account, calculate nutrition and weight progress, analyze meals you submit, validate subscriptions, prevent abuse, provide support, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, or use health or fitness data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Service providers

PEAKR uses Apple and Google for authentication, Supabase for authentication/database/private image storage/server functions, RevenueCat for subscription status, PostHog for product analytics, Replicate and its underlying model providers for meal analysis requested through protected server functions, and Resend for transactional account emails.

HealthKit step data is read only after permission, used to display your activity progress, and is not sent to meal-analysis providers, advertising services, or data brokers. Meal descriptions and meal photos are sent for AI analysis only when you explicitly request that feature.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Supabase protects account data with row-level access controls and encryption in transit. Food and progress photos are stored in private buckets. Session tokens use the iOS Keychain. Account data is kept while your account is active or as legally necessary. From Settings → Delete account, you can remove user-owned database rows, private photos, the authentication account, pending account emails, and local app data. This does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Your rights and contact

Política de Privacidad de PEAKR

PEAKR trata los datos de cuenta facilitados mediante Apple o Google, los datos de nutrición, peso y pasos que conectas o introduces, las fotos que seleccionas expresamente, el estado de tu suscripción y eventos técnicos necesarios para prestar y mejorar el servicio. No vendemos datos, no mostramos publicidad de terceros y no usamos datos de salud o fitness para seguimiento publicitario.

Los pasos de HealthKit se leen únicamente con tu permiso y no se envían a proveedores de análisis de comidas, publicidad ni intermediarios de datos. Las descripciones y fotos de comidas se procesan con IA solo cuando solicitas expresamente esa función.

Puedes eliminar tu cuenta y sus datos desde Ajustes → Eliminar cuenta. Para ejercer derechos de acceso, rectificación, portabilidad, oposición o supresión, escribe a peakr.support@gmail.com.
