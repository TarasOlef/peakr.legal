PEAKR Support

For account, subscription, food analysis, or privacy help, email peakr.support@gmail.com. Include the app version and a short description. Never send passwords or full payment details.

Subscriptions

Restore purchases from PEAKR Settings. Manage or cancel an Apple subscription from Apple Account subscription settings.

Account deletion

Open PEAKR → Settings → Delete account. This removes the PEAKR account and user-owned data, but does not cancel an Apple subscription.
