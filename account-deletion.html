Delete your PEAKR account

Open PEAKR → Settings → Delete account and confirm the request. The app deletes your authentication account, profile, meals, nutrition and weight progress, private meal and progress photos, pending account emails, and local app data.

Deleting your PEAKR account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage the subscription separately in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Need help?

Email peakr.support@gmail.com. Never send passwords or full payment details.

Eliminar tu cuenta de PEAKR

Abre PEAKR → Ajustes → Eliminar cuenta y confirma la solicitud. La app elimina tu cuenta de autenticación, perfil, comidas, progreso nutricional y de peso, fotos privadas, correos pendientes y datos locales.

Eliminar la cuenta de PEAKR no cancela una suscripción de Apple. Gestiona la suscripción por separado desde los ajustes de suscripciones de tu cuenta de Apple.
